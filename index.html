EndLife

Every death in the game. One elegant menu.

Click any Sim → End Life... → choose from the game's complete catalogue of demises: poison, Murphy bed, vicious killer rabbit, broken heart, and everything else your packs provide. Full death scenes, Grim Reaper included. Your own Sim walks away whistling.

  • 🎯 The clicked Sim dies — never your storyteller
  • 🐶 Pets exempt; babies, infants & toddlers protected
  • 🧳 Visitors and walk-bys are valid victims
  • 📦 No packs required — the menu adapts to what you own
  • ⌨️ Console commands for off-lot arrangements

Installing mods

  1. Unzip into Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 4\Mods
  2. Game Options → Other → ✔ Script Mods Allowed
  3. Restart the game. Dag dag!
